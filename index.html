The cost of waiting

"Let's wait until next quarter" is a decision too.

Doing nothing feels safe. In technology, it usually means someone else makes the decision for you.

  • The vendor sets the timeline.Their quarter-end becomes your deadline, and their engineers design what they sell.
  • Contracts renew on old terms.Notice windows close quietly; multi-year renewals lock in at last year's price and leverage.
  • AI spreads without rules.Staff adopt tools on their own, and your data goes with them.
  • Good people leave a leadership vacuum.Your strongest IT staff are the first to get recruited away.
  • Integration costs compound.Every month two systems run side by side is a month you pay for both.
